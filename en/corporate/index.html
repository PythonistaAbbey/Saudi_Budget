Leasing benefits

Verified public infoSource: budgetsaudi.com

Payments 20%–30% lower than for a purchase loan for the same car and same term

Refresh vehicles every two to four years, depending on lease length

No down payment option · Credit facility of 30 days

Unlimited mileage on all lease cars without any excess mileage charges during the lease tenure

Periodic maintenance included; first service at 5,000 km, then every 15,000 km (petrol) or 14,000 km (diesel); tyres roughly every 50,000 km

Free towing within 350 km of any Budget office. Immediate replacement vehicles after accidents or breakdowns.

Comprehensive cover incl. unlimited third-party cover up to SAR 10 million; minimum deductible SAR 2,500

New vehicles delivered within 15 working days (plus up to 9 working days if registration is delayed); subject to dealer availability
